THE RIGHT CARE, AT THE RIGHT TIME

Every story below is a randomized trial, and each one is about a decision. Which prevention treatment to give after a stroke. How much oxygen a patient needs, minute by minute. Whether a kidney lesion needs an operation. Which patients with a positive blood test should see a specialist first. In each case AI helped, and patients got better-matched care: fewer new strokes, steadier oxygen, fewer needless operations and faster diagnoses.
